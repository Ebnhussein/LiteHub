Rules

  • One primary (gradient) button per view; everything else is secondary. Danger only for destructive actions.
  • Brand 2 appears only as the end of the brand gradient; text and thin lines use Brand (light) or Brand ink (dark).
  • Cards are flat grouped surfaces (rounded 18, iOS separator hairline); glass is reserved for floating chrome (side bar, quick switcher). Controls are capsules; switches are green with a white knob.
  • Status is never color-only: unread has a count, running has a dot plus the "Running" label in Performance.
  • New screens use Ds.PageX/PageTop, Ds.Title, Ui.Row cards and Ui.Btn; do not add raw colors or radii.
